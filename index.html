Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This application ("Hermes Automation") is a private, single-user automation tool operated solely by its owner. It syncs and backs up files to and from Google Drive and manages the owner's own Gmail, Calendar, and Google Workspace documents.

Data Accessed

The application accesses Google Drive files, Gmail messages, Calendar events, and Google Workspace documents belonging to the account owner. Access is granted by the owner through Google's standard OAuth consent flow and can be revoked at any time.

How Data Is Used

Data is used exclusively to perform the owner's own automation tasks — syncing a personal notes vault, creating backups, and sending notifications to the owner. Data is never sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any third party.

Data Storage

All data remains within the owner's own Google account and the owner's private server. No data is transmitted to external parties, and no analytics or tracking is performed.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed for the automation tasks. The owner may revoke the application's access at any time via the Google Account permissions page, which immediately terminates all access.

Contact

This is a private, single-user application. Questions may be directed to the application's registered support contact.

Hermes Automation — personal use only.
